Main Screen

The main screen follows whatever is playing on your Plex server and shows synced lyrics in real time.

  • Highlight — highlights the currently-sung line.
  • Sync — nudge lyrics earlier or later if they're off beat. Hover to reveal fine tuning (-1s to +1s). Click the 0.00s readout to reset. Your adjustment is remembered per song.
  • Auto-Scroll — keeps the current line centered.
  • Countdown — switches the pre-lyrics countdown between Bar (small progress bar) and Big (full-screen celebratory ring overlay, no numbers).
  • Trivia — pop-up music trivia during long instrumental breaks.
  • Vis — cycles animated shader backgrounds (Geiss, MilkDrop, Void, Nebula, Rings).
  • Live — audio-reactive visuals driven by your microphone. See Visualizers below.
  • OBS — minimal transparent lyric display for streaming. Pair with fullscreen and add as a browser source in OBS.
  • Explicit/Clean — filters explicit words from lyrics.
  • Romaji — romanizes Japanese lyrics.
  • Fullscreen — fullscreen mode. The top bar auto-hides when idle.

Click the ✏️ next to a song title to correct the artist/title when the wrong lyrics load. Your correction is saved.

Visualizers

Shader visualizers (Vis button)

Lightweight animated backgrounds. They render at reduced resolution and auto-drop quality if your GPU struggles.

Live visualizers (Live button)

These react to the actual music via your microphone.

Microphone needs a secure context. Open LyricDeck over the https address (not the local http://192.168… IP), then allow mic access when asked.
  • Style — switch between MilkDrop (real MilkDrop presets, auto-rotating), Orbs (bass-reactive bouncing orbs), Chasers (orbiting lights), and Spectrum (classic FFT bars). Orbs, Chasers, and Spectrum also react subtly to your mouse.
  • Next > — skip to the next MilkDrop preset.
  • Cycle — auto-change MilkDrop presets every 30 seconds.
  • Lock — freeze on the current preset and remember it for next time.
  • Ban — never show the current preset again. Ban the frantic ones, lock the good ones, and your rotation gets better over time.

Party Mode /party

Put this on the big screen. Huge current lyric line, dimmed next-line preview, track header. Follows the music by itself, no login needed on the display once the main screen has connected once.

In duet mode, the current line is color-coded by singer part (cyan = A, pink = B).

Phone Remote /remote

  • Search for songs and add them to the party queue.
  • See what's playing and manage the queue.
  • Duet controls — see Duet Mode below.

Duet Mode

Split a song's vocal parts between two singers, each following on their own phone.

  • On any phone at /remote, tap Duet: ON.
  • Tap lyric lines to assign them: both → A → B. Assignments are saved per song.
  • Each singer taps I sing A or I sing B — their lines are highlighted, the other's dimmed.
  • The main screen and party display color-code parts automatically (cyan = A, pink = B).

OBS Overlay /overlay

Transparent lyric overlay for streaming. Add as a browser source in OBS. Or use the OBS toggle on the main screen instead — same thing, no second URL needed.

Karaoke night setup: /party on the TV, /remote on everyone's phones for the queue, Live visualizers behind the lyrics, duet mode for the brave.

Troubleshooting

  • Lyrics out of sync — hover the sync pill and nudge with the fine-tune buttons. It's saved per song.
  • Wrong lyrics loaded — click ✏️ by the title and correct the artist/title.
  • Live visualizers won't start — use the https address, not the local IP. Mic access requires a secure context.
  • Party screen stuck on an old song — open the main screen once so it picks up the current session, party mode follows from there.
